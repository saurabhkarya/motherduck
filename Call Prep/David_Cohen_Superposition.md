## Anticipated Objections

### "My clients are consultants, not end-users"
```
YOU: "Exactly—that's why this is interesting.

Your consultant clients need tools to recommend to THEIR
clients. If you help them understand when to recommend
MotherDuck vs. Snowflake, they deliver better advice.

It's another way you're adding value to their toolkit."
```

### "I focus on business strategy, not technical tools"
```
YOU: "Totally get it. But platform selection impacts
the projects your consultants scope and sell.

If they recommend an expensive platform, the project
costs more, the client hesitates. If they recommend
something simpler and cheaper, they close more deals.

It's a business strategy issue disguised as a technical
decision."
```

### "What's in it for me?"
```
YOU: "A few things:

1. You become more valuable to your consultant clients
   by helping them with platform recommendations
2. We could co-create content for your audience
3. If there's a referral structure that makes sense,
   we're open to exploring that

What would be most valuable to you?"
```

### "I've never heard of MotherDuck"
```
YOU: "Fair—we're newer but not inexperienced. Founded by
the team behind Google BigQuery, backed by a16z.

We're becoming the go-to for consultants who work with
mid-market clients. Given your positioning, you'd
probably want to know about us before your competitors do."
```
