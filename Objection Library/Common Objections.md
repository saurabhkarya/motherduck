# Common Objections and Responses

## Competitor Mentions

### "We're using Snowflake"
**Pattern from calls:** Often hear "happy with Snowflake ecosystem"
**Response:** "Totally understand - many of our customers use MotherDuck alongside Snowflake for specific use cases. What drew you to check out MotherDuck?"
**When to DQ:** If they mention "happy with Snowflake, no budget for change"

### "We're on Databricks"
**Response:** "Makes sense for your ML workloads. MotherDuck is often used for ad-hoc analytics alongside existing warehouses."

### "Using Microsoft Fabric"
**Pattern:** Often from orgs without existing DWH knowledge
**Note:** Microsoft lock-in prevents vendor switching

## Volume/Size Concerns

### "Too small for us"
**DQ Signals:**
- Under 5GB data
- 2 employees or fewer
- Personal/hobby project

### "Volume too low"
**Response:** "At what scale would a serverless warehouse make sense?"

## Use Case Objections

### "Just learning/curiosity"
**DQ immediately if:**
- Student term project
- Teacher demo
- Personal learning only

**Nurture if:**
- Professional exploring for future work use

### "Personal project"
**Questions to qualify:**
- "Is this for a company or side project?"
- "Are you evaluating for your team?"

## Timing/Budget

### "No budget"
**Follow-up:** "Is this a timing issue or a priority issue?"

### "Not right now"
**Response:** "When would be a better time to revisit this?"
**Set nurture for:** 30/60/90 days based on response

## Process Objections

### "Need to check with team"
**Response:** "Happy to include your team on a quick demo. Who else would be involved in evaluating this?"

## Patterns from Call Analysis

### Successful Deal Signals
- 200GB+ data
- Multiple users/locations
- Current pain with existing solution
- Specific use case (retail analytics, warehousing data)

### Common DQ Patterns
- Students/teachers
- Personal projects without business use
- Very small data volumes (<1GB)
- Happy with current competitor
- No budget and no timeline
