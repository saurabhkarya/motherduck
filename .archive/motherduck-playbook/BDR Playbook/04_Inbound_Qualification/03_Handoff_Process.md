# AE Handoff Process

A smooth handoff sets the AE up for success and creates a great prospect experience. Here's how to do it right.

---

## SDR/AE Hot Leads Daily Alignment

### Daily Workflow
1. **SDR sends AE top leads of the day**
   - Post in shared channel or direct message
   - If lead is "double-top-tier", send to Scott as well

2. **Goal: Bear-Hug on LinkedIn**
   - Multiple MotherDuckers reach out via LinkedIn
   - SDR handles email/sequencing
   - AE handles LinkedIn engagement
   - Creates multi-touch, high-value impression

### What Makes a "Top Lead"
- Large company (100+ employees)
- Strong use case identified
- High-value persona (VP Data, Head of Engineering)
- Competitive situation (actively on Snowflake/Databricks)
- Time-sensitive need

## SDR Pre-Meeting Checklist

When a meeting is booked, complete these tasks BEFORE the discovery call:

### HubSpot Setup
- [ ] Create new HubSpot deal
- [ ] Mark deal as "Pre-Qualification"
- [ ] Insert pre-meeting notes into deal ("Notes" tab)
- [ ] Use SDR Notes Snippet for consistency

### Team Communication
- [ ] Send pre-meeting notes to AE + CE via Slack
- [ ] Include: Company, Role, Use Case/Need, Stack, Goals

### Prospect Communication
- [ ] Intro AE and CE to prospect via email prior to call
- [ ] Confirm meeting time with prospect

### SDR Notes Snippet Template
```
**Company:** [Company Name]
**Role:** [Prospect's Title]
**Use Case / Need:** [What they're trying to solve]
**Stack:** [Current tech - Snowflake, BigQuery, DuckDB, etc.]
**Goals for the meeting:** [What you want to accomplish]
```

## AE Post-Call Workflow

### Same-Day Requirements
AE must update HubSpot by EOD on the day of the meeting:
- Mark deal as **(1) Qualified (Accepted)** OR
- Mark deal as **(2) Closed Lost (Rejected)**

### Primary Qualification Criteria for Accept/Reject

**At least 1 of these 3 must be met (not all required):**

| Criteria | Threshold |
|----------|-----------|
| Company Size | 25+ employees |
| Use Case | Project/initiative identified |
| Tech Stack | Data ecosystem/tools identified |

## Asking Technical Questions

When you need technical answers for a prospect, use #ask-hatchery with this format:

### Required Information
1. **Context**: Include all relevant context on prospect, use case, account
2. **Lead Urgency**: Tier the lead:
   - (1) Above average
   - (2) Average
   - (3) Below average
3. **Timeline Urgency**: When do you need the answer?
   - "A few hours from now"
   - "Tomorrow"
   - "This week"

### Example Request
```
Hey team, need some help with a technical question:

**Context:** Prospect is a 200-person fintech evaluating MD for
real-time portfolio analytics. Currently on Snowflake, spending
~$50k/month. Concerned about latency for sub-second queries.

**Lead Urgency:** (1) Above average - strong fit, active eval

**Timeline:** Need to respond by tomorrow morning

**Question:** Can MotherDuck handle sub-second queries on 500GB
with 50 concurrent users? Any benchmarks I can share?
```

## Handoff Checklist

Before scheduling the AE meeting, confirm:

- [ ] **Pain confirmed**: Clear problem MotherDuck solves
- [ ] **Fit validated**: Company matches ICP
- [ ] **Budget indication**: Exists or can be created
- [ ] **Authority mapped**: Know who decides
- [ ] **Timeline known**: Actively looking (not "someday")
- [ ] **Notes complete**: All discovery logged in CRM
- [ ] **Demo scheduled**: Calendar invite sent

## The Handoff Meeting Types

### Option 1: Warm Handoff (Preferred)
You join the first 5-10 minutes of the AE call.

**Pros**:
- Personal introduction
- Prospect feels taken care of
- You can clarify context live

**How to do it**:
```
"[Prospect], I'd like to introduce you to [AE Name], one of our 
solutions engineers. They're going to dive deeper into your 
use case and show you how MotherDuck works in detail.

[AE Name], [Prospect] is the [Title] at [Company]. They're 
currently on [Snowflake] and looking to [reduce costs / move faster]. 
The main pain points we discussed were [X, Y, Z].

I'll let you two take it from here—[Prospect], you're in great hands!"
```

### Option 2: Email Introduction
When schedules don't align.

**Template**:
```
Subject: Intro: [AE Name] <> [Prospect Name] | MotherDuck

Hi [Prospect],

As promised, I'm connecting you with [AE Name], who will be your 
main point of contact for the technical evaluation.

[AE Name]—[Prospect] is the [Title] at [Company]. They're 
currently using [Snowflake] and exploring options to:
- [Pain point 1]
- [Pain point 2]

I've shared my notes from our call in [CRM], including the 
specific use cases [Prospect] wants to explore.

You two are scheduled for [Date/Time]. Looking forward to 
hearing how it goes!

Best,
[Your Name]
```

### Option 3: Async Handoff
When prospect books directly with AE.

**Internal Slack/Email to AE**:
```
Hey [AE Name],

You have a demo with [Prospect Name] from [Company] on [Date].

Quick context:
- Company: [Size] employees, [Industry], [Funding stage]
- Current stack: [Snowflake/BigQuery/etc]
- Pain: [Top 1-2 pain points]
- Timeline: [When they want to decide]
- Decision maker: [Who decides] - [attending/not attending]

My read: [Your gut on deal quality and what to focus on]

Full notes in [CRM link].

Let me know if you need anything else!
```

## Information to Always Include

### Company Context
- Company name, size, industry
- Funding stage and recent news
- What they do (1 sentence)

### Technical Context
- Current data warehouse
- Data team size and structure
- Data volume (if known)
- Key tools in their stack

### Pain & Goals
- Top 2-3 pain points
- Impact of those pains
- What success looks like for them

### Buying Context
- Who's the decision maker
- Who else is involved
- Budget situation
- Timeline and urgency
- Any known objections

### Call Context
- How did they find us
- Key quotes from discovery
- What to focus on in demo
- What to avoid or be careful about

## Post-Handoff Responsibilities

### Your Responsibilities
| Task | Timing |
|------|--------|
| Send handoff notes | Before AE meeting |
| Introduce via email (if no warm handoff) | Before AE meeting |
| Log handoff in CRM | Day of handoff |
| Check in with AE | 1-2 days after demo |

### What You Don't Do
- Don't continue prospecting this account
- Don't schedule additional meetings yourself
- Don't respond to prospect questions (forward to AE)
- Don't negotiate or discuss pricing

### When to Re-Engage
You may re-engage if:
- AE asks you to (e.g., prospect went dark)
- Opportunity is closed-lost and recycled
- New contacts at the same company

---

## Handoff Quality Standards

### Great Handoff (Aim for this)
- AE has everything they need
- Prospect feels continuity
- No repeated questions
- Demo is focused and relevant

### Acceptable Handoff
- Key information provided
- Some gaps AE can fill
- Minor friction but workable

### Poor Handoff (Avoid)
- Missing critical info
- Prospect confused about next steps
- AE unprepared
- Repeated discovery questions

---

## Common Handoff Mistakes

| Mistake | Impact | Fix |
|---------|--------|-----|
| Handing off too early | Wasted AE time | Complete qualification first |
| Incomplete notes | AE flies blind | Use the template, every time |
| No warm intro | Prospect feels passed off | Join first 5 min or send intro email |
| Wrong meeting type | Bad prospect experience | Clarify what they'll get |
| Not following up | Deals fall through cracks | Check in with AE after demo |

## Meeting Scheduling Best Practices

### Calendar Invite Details
```
Subject: MotherDuck Demo - [Company Name]

Hi [Prospect],

Looking forward to our call! Here's what we'll cover:

• Quick intro and context
• Live demo of MotherDuck focused on [their use case]
• Your questions
• Next steps if there's a fit

Attendees:
• [Prospect Name], [Title] - [Company]
• [AE Name], Solutions Engineer - MotherDuck
• [Your Name], [Your Title] - MotherDuck (first 5 min)

Zoom: [link]

See you then!
```

### Timing Tips
- 45-60 minutes for demos (not 30)
- Tuesday-Thursday preferred
- Avoid Monday mornings, Friday afternoons
- Give 24-48 hours notice minimum

### Reminders
- Send calendar invite immediately
- Send reminder email day before
- Confirm attendees 1 day out
- Have backup if prospect needs to reschedule

- Ask AEs for feedback after demos
