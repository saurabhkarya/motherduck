# Qualification Frameworks

Use these frameworks to systematically qualify opportunities and prioritize your time.

---

## BANT Framework

The classic framework. Good for quick qualification.

| Criteria | Questions | Good Signals | Bad Signals |
|----------|-----------|--------------|-------------|
| **B**udget | "Is there budget for this?" | "Yes" / "We'd find it" | "No budget" / "Free tier only" |
| **A**uthority | "Who decides?" | Talking to DM or strong influencer | Junior IC, no influence |
| **N**eed | "What problem are you solving?" | Clear pain, specific use case | "Just exploring" |
| **T**imeline | "When do you need this?" | Next 1-3 months | "Someday" / "Not sure" |

### BANT Scoring
| Score | Meaning | Action |
|-------|---------|--------|
| 4/4 | Hot lead | Fast-track to AE |
| 3/4 | Qualified | Schedule demo |
| 2/4 | Developing | Nurture, stay in touch |
| 1/4 | Unqualified | Deprioritize |

## MEDDIC Framework

More rigorous. Use for larger opportunities.

| Criteria | Definition | Questions |
|----------|------------|-----------|
| **M**etrics | Quantified business impact | "What would success look like in numbers?" |
| **E**conomic Buyer | Person with budget authority | "Who signs off on the budget?" |
| **D**ecision Criteria | How they'll evaluate | "What factors matter most in your decision?" |
| **D**ecision Process | Steps to purchase | "Walk me through how decisions like this get made." |
| **I**dentify Pain | The problem to solve | "What's the #1 problem you're trying to solve?" |
| **C**hampion | Internal advocate | "Who's most invested in solving this?" |

### MEDDIC Qualification Checklist
- [ ] Can articulate specific metrics/goals
- [ ] Know who the economic buyer is
- [ ] Understand their decision criteria
- [ ] Mapped the decision process
- [ ] Identified clear, urgent pain
- [ ] Have an internal champion

---

## CHAMP Framework

Reverses BANT to lead with Challenges.

| Criteria | Focus | Questions |
|----------|-------|-----------|
| **CH**allenges | Problems they face | "What's not working today?" |
| **A**uthority | Who's involved | "Who needs to be part of this decision?" |
| **M**oney | Budget reality | "How do you typically budget for tools like this?" |
| **P**rioritization | Where this ranks | "How does this compare to other priorities?" |

## Qualification Questions by Stage

### Initial Inquiry (Email/Chat)
Quick questions to assess basic fit:
1. "What prompted you to reach out?"
2. "What are you using for your data warehouse today?"
3. "What are you hoping to accomplish?"

### First Call (15 min)
Deeper qualification:
1. "Tell me about your current data stack."
2. "What's working? What's not?"
3. "Who else would be involved in evaluating a new tool?"
4. "What's your timeline for making a change?"

### Demo Qualification
Confirm before scheduling demo:
1. "Can you bring the technical decision-maker to the demo?"
2. "What specific use cases should we focus on?"
3. "What would make this demo successful for you?"

## Opportunity Stages

| Stage | Criteria | Exit Criteria |
|-------|----------|---------------|
| **Inquiry** | Initial contact | Responded, basic info collected |
| **Qualified Lead** | BANT confirmed | AE accepts handoff |
| **Discovery** | Deep-dive call completed | Pain, impact, process mapped |
| **Demo/Evaluation** | Technical demo delivered | Positive feedback, trial interest |
| **Trial** | POC underway | Success criteria met |
| **Negotiation** | Pricing discussion | Terms agreed |
| **Closed Won** | Contract signed | 🎉 |

## Handoff Criteria

Only hand off to AE when:

- [ ] Confirmed pain point that MotherDuck solves
- [ ] Budget exists or can be found
- [ ] Decision maker identified (ideally involved)
- [ ] Timeline within 6 months
- [ ] Next step agreed (demo scheduled)
- [ ] Complete notes in CRM
