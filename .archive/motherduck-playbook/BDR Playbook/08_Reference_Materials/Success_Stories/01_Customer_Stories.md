# Customer Success Stories

Real examples of MotherDuck customers and their results. Use these in sales conversations to build credibility and demonstrate value.

## Featured Success Stories

### Definite: 70% Cost Reduction

**Company**: Definite (Y Combinator startup)
**Industry**: Data Infrastructure
**Previous Solution**: Traditional cloud data warehouse

**Challenge**:
Definite was paying significant costs for their data warehouse while not fully utilizing the provisioned capacity.

**Solution**:
Switched to MotherDuck's DuckDB-based architecture.

**Results**:
| Metric | Impact |
|--------|--------|
| **Cost Reduction** | 70% |
| **Infrastructure Management** | Eliminated |

**Quote to Use**:
> "We saw a 70% cost reduction by moving from a provisioned warehouse to a DuckDB-based architecture."

**When to Reference**:
- Cost-sensitive prospects
- Startups watching burn rate
- Companies frustrated with over-provisioning

### FinQore: 60x Performance Improvement

**Company**: FinQore
**Industry**: Fintech
**Use Case**: ETL pipelines, data processing

**Challenge**:
FinQore's ETL pipelines were taking 8 hours to complete, creating bottlenecks in their data workflows.

**Solution**:
Rebuilt ETL pipelines using DuckDB and MotherDuck as core components.

**Results**:

|--------|--------|-------|-------------|

**Quote to Use**:

**When to Reference**:
- ETL/pipeline bottlenecks
- Fintech companies
- Performance-focused conversations

## How to Use Customer Stories

### In Cold Outreach
```
Subject: How [Similar Company] cut warehouse costs 70%

Hi [First Name],

Saw you're [building/growing] at [Company]. Quick story—

Definite (YC startup) switched to MotherDuck and cut their 
data warehouse costs by 70%. No infrastructure to manage, 
just SQL.

If cost or complexity is a pain point, happy to share more.

[Your Name]
```

### In Discovery Calls
```
"You mentioned [cost/speed/complexity] is a challenge. That's 
actually why a lot of companies come to us. For example, 
FinQore was spending 8 hours on ETL jobs that now take 8 
minutes with MotherDuck. Would something like that help?"
```

### In Objection Handling
```
Objection: "Is MotherDuck proven?"

Response: "Great question. Companies like [Definite/Gardyn/FinQore] 
are running production workloads with us. Definite saw 70% cost 
savings, FinQore got 60x faster ETL. We're SOC 2 Type II certified 
and production-ready."
```

### In Demos
```
"Before I show you the product, let me share a quick story. 
[Customer] was in a similar situation—[pain point]. After 
switching to MotherDuck, they [result]. Let me show you 
how that works..."
```
