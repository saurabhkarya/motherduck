# Disqualification Criteria

Knowing when to walk away is as important as knowing who to pursue. Time spent on bad-fit prospects is time not spent on good ones.

---

## Hard Disqualifiers

These are non-negotiable. Move on immediately.

| Criteria | Why It's a Disqualifier |
|----------|-------------------------|
| **No data/analytics use case** | Nothing to solve for |
| **Strict on-premises requirement** | MotherDuck is cloud-only |
| **Petabyte+ scale, real-time streaming** | Need enterprise solutions |
| **No technical resources** | Can't implement or maintain |
| **Not willing to evaluate** | No path forward |

---

## Soft Disqualifiers

These require judgment. May be worth nurturing but not priority.

### Budget Concerns
| Signal | Action |
|--------|--------|
| "We have zero budget this year" | Nurture for next quarter |
| "We can only afford free tier" | Point to free plan, check back |
| "Need to go through lengthy procurement" | Longer cycle, stay in touch |

### Timing Issues
| Signal | Action |
|--------|--------|
| "We just signed a 3-year Snowflake deal" | Note renewal date, nurture |
| "Major migration underway" | Wait until stabilized |
| "New data leader starting in 3 months" | Reconnect when they start |
| "Not a priority this quarter" | Set follow-up for next quarter |

### Fit Concerns
| Signal | Action |
|--------|--------|
| "Our data is 100+ TB" | Explore specific use case, may still fit |
| "We need heavy ML/AI" | Position as analytics layer, not ML platform |
| "We're married to Spark" | Databricks may be better fit |
| "We need CDC/streaming" | Explore batch alternatives |

---

## Disqualification Questions

Ask these early to avoid wasting time:

### Budget & Authority
- "Do you have budget allocated for data infrastructure this year?"
- "Who else would be involved in a decision like this?"
- "What's your typical evaluation process for new tools?"

### Timing
- "When are you looking to make a change?"
- "Is this a priority for the next quarter?"
- "Are there any constraints on your timeline?"

### Fit
- "How much data are we talking about?"
- "Do you have a technical team to implement and maintain this?"
- "Are there any compliance requirements we should know about?"

## The BANT Check

Quick qualification framework:

| Criteria | Good Sign | Bad Sign |
|----------|-----------|----------|
| **B**udget | Has allocated budget | "No budget" / "Maybe next year" |
| **A**uthority | Speaking to decision maker or strong influencer | Junior IC with no influence |
| **N**eed | Clear pain point, active search | "Just curious" / No urgency |
| **T**imeline | Decision in < 3 months | "Someday" / "Not a priority" |

**Scoring**:
- 4/4: Hot lead, fast-track
- 3/4: Qualified, pursue
- 2/4: Nurture, stay in touch
- 1/4 or less: Deprioritize

## Red Flag Phrases

When you hear these, probe deeper:

| Phrase | What It Might Mean | Follow-up |
|--------|-------------------|-----------|
| "Just doing research" | No real intent | "What prompted the research?" |
| "Checking boxes for RFP" | Incumbent advantage | "Who's the current vendor?" |
| "We love Snowflake" | High switching cost | "What made you take this call?" |
| "Our CTO won't change" | Blocker | "Has your CTO evaluated MotherDuck?" |
| "We need everything" | Unrealistic expectations | "What's the #1 priority?" |
| "Can you send pricing?" | Avoiding conversation | "Happy to! What's driving your interest?" |

## Graceful Exit Scripts

### Not the Right Fit
> "Thanks for sharing more about your setup. It sounds like [X] might actually be a better fit for your needs right now. If anything changes, feel free to reach back out."

### Bad Timing
> "I understand the timing isn't right. I'll make a note to reconnect in [timeframe]. In the meantime, feel free to explore our free tier."

### No Budget
> "Totally understand budget constraints. Our free tier includes 10GB and 10 compute hours—enough to test the waters. Want me to send you the signup link?"

### No Authority
> "Thanks for flagging that. Would it make sense to loop in [decision maker] for a quick intro? I want to make sure we're talking to the right people."

---

## When to Re-engage

| Disqualification Reason | Re-engage When |
|------------------------|----------------|
| Budget issues | New fiscal year, new funding round |
| Just signed with competitor | 6 months before renewal |
| Timing not right | Next quarter, or when trigger event occurs |
| Wrong person | Find the right contact |
| Company too small | Headcount grows, funding raised |
