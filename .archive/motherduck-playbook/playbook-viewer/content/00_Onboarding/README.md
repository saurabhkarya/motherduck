# MotherDuck Sales Onboarding Program

Welcome to the Flock! This 5-week onboarding program is designed to take new BDRs and AEs from limited technical knowledge to confident, credible conversations with those in the data ecosystem.

---

## The MotherDuck Way

- **What blocks the way, becomes the way.**
- **Don't make assumptions.**
- **Assume positive intent.**
- **Make your mark.**

### Learning Philosophy
Go wide before going deep. You don't need to be a data engineer, but you need to understand how the pieces fit together and where MotherDuck adds value.

> "We have to continually be jumping off cliffs and developing our wings on the way down." - Vonnegut

## The Comprehensive Exam

At the end of Week 3, you'll complete the MotherDuck Comprehensive Exam.

**Purpose**: It's designed to be difficult and help pinpoint gaps and areas for focus. It shows where you might feel out of your depth with customers.

**Philosophy**: The point is not to ace the exam - it's to learn from it and provide an honest lens for your own development.

**No surprises**: The exam questions are provided upfront so you can prepare.

## Imprinting at MotherDuck

> Ducks are exceptional animals. They can swim, fly, walk, and some claim they can even run. Upon hatching, fledgling ducks go through the process of "imprinting" where they attach to the first moving object they see and copy its every move.

You are in the process of "imprinting" at MotherDuck. Be intentional with this period of time, and be aware that you are forming the foundation of what comes next and what you will build upon.

**Ducks can fly!**
