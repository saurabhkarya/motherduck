## Days 1-3: Prospecting is the Game

### The Outbound Mindset

> Open source gives us a big audience, but it doesn't hand us pipeline. We have to go out and find the real opportunities.

In a consumption model, nothing is truly "closed" - every customer is just a set of future workloads you haven't uncovered yet.

The only way to stay ahead is to:
- Keep talking to people
- Keep surfacing projects
- Keep replacing what you just won

**Reality Check**: Inbound is nice, community love is nice, but **disciplined, everyday prospecting will change our trajectory**.

### Core Concepts

#### Account Books
| Book Type | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Community Signals** | Leads from community engagement |
| **Recycling** | Previously engaged leads to re-engage |
| **Look-a-Like** | Companies similar to successful customers |

| Activity | Purpose |
|----------|---------|
| Sourcing Companies & Contacts | Building your target list |
| Leveraging LinkedIn | Using the MotherDuck Network |

## Accepting an Opportunity

Post-meeting, update the deal in HubSpot:

### Tag the Account

| Tag | Definition |
|-----|------------|
| **Key** | One of your top accounts that you will really focus on |
| **Core** | The majority of accounts you will work |
| **Starter** | Likely only need free plan or unlikely to reach $6k annual consumption |

### Mark the Deal Stage
- **Qualified** - Moving forward
- **Closed Lost** - Not moving forward

## Your Weekly Rhythm

### Monday
- [ ] Review pipeline and priorities for the week
- [ ] Check all notification channels
- [ ] Plan outbound targets for the week

### Tuesday - Thursday
- [ ] Morning: Work inbound leads (signups, demand gen)
- [ ] Midday: Outbound prospecting blocks
- [ ] Afternoon: Follow-ups and responses
- [ ] End of day: Update HubSpot

### Friday
- [ ] Clear task queue
- [ ] Review week's activities and results
- [ ] Plan for next week
- [ ] Post notable leads

### Daily Non-Negotiables
- [ ] Check all HubSpot contact views
- [ ] Respond to all inbound within 4 hours
- [ ] Make minimum 10 calls
- [ ] Send minimum 15 LinkedIn connections
- [ ] Post notable leads to #team-sales
