# SOC 2 Type II Compliance: What It Means for Sales

This document explains SOC 2 Type II compliance in the context of selling MotherDuck to enterprise and security-conscious customers.

---

## What is SOC 2?

**SOC 2** (Service Organization Control 2) is a security framework developed by the American Institute of CPAs (AICPA) that specifies how organizations should manage customer data.

### The Trust Service Criteria

SOC 2 evaluates organizations against five "Trust Service Criteria":

| Criteria | What It Covers |
|----------|----------------|
| **Security** | Protection against unauthorized access |
| **Availability** | System uptime and accessibility |
| **Processing Integrity** | Accurate and timely data processing |
| **Confidentiality** | Protection of sensitive information |
| **Privacy** | Personal information handling |

---

## Type I vs. Type II: The Key Difference

| Type | What It Means | Duration | Value |
|------|---------------|----------|-------|
| **Type I** | Controls are *designed* properly | Point-in-time snapshot | Basic assurance |
| **Type II** | Controls *work effectively* over time | 6-12 month audit period | Strong assurance |

**Key Talking Point**:
> "Type II is the gold standard. It proves our security controls don't just exist on paper—they've been tested and validated by independent auditors over an extended period."

## Who Cares About SOC 2?

### High Priority (Lead with Compliance)

| Industry | Why They Care |
|----------|---------------|
| **Fintech** | Regulatory requirements, customer trust |
| **Healthcare** | Often paired with HIPAA, patient data |
| **Enterprise SaaS** | Vendor management policies |
| **Government/Public Sector** | Compliance mandates |

### Medium Priority (Mention if Asked)

| Industry | Why They Care |
|----------|---------------|
| **E-commerce** | PCI-adjacent, customer data |
| **B2B Software** | Enterprise customer requirements |
| **Consulting/Professional Services** | Client data protection |

### Lower Priority (Usually Not Asked)

| Industry | Why |
|----------|-----|
| **Early-stage startups** | Security review not yet formalized |
| **Internal tools only** | No external customer data |
