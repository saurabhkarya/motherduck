## Cold Email Best Practices

### Subject Lines That Work
- Questions: "Using DuckDB?" / "Snowflake costs too high?"
- Specifics: "70% cheaper than Snowflake"
- Personal: "[Mutual connection] suggested I reach out"
- Curiosity: "Quick question about your data stack"

### Subject Lines to Avoid
- Generic: "Quick question" / "Following up"
- Salesy: "EXCLUSIVE OFFER" / "Don't miss out"
- Long: Keep under 50 characters

### Email Body Rules
1. **Keep it short**: Under 100 words
2. **One CTA**: Don't give multiple options
3. **Personalize**: Reference something specific
4. **Value first**: Lead with what's in it for them
5. **No attachments**: They kill deliverability

### Personalization Triggers
- Recent funding round
- New hire (especially data roles)
- Job posting (data engineer, analyst)
- Blog post or tweet about data
- Conference talk
- Company milestone
