# LinkedIn Outreach Playbook

LinkedIn is often more effective than email for technical personas. Here's how to use it effectively.

---

## LinkedIn Profile Optimization

Before outreach, optimize your profile:

### Headline
Bad: "BDR at MotherDuck"
Good: "Helping data teams move faster with serverless analytics | MotherDuck"

### About Section
```
I help data engineers and analysts spend less time on infrastructure 
and more time on insights.

MotherDuck is a serverless cloud data warehouse built on DuckDB—the 
fastest-growing analytical database. No clusters to manage, 70% cheaper 
than Snowflake.

Always happy to chat about:
• Modern data stack trends
• DuckDB use cases
• Serverless analytics

DM me or book time: [calendly link]
```

### Activity
- Share DuckDB/data content weekly
- Comment on prospects' posts
- Engage in data communities

## Connection Request Templates

### Template 1: The Mutual Interest
```
Hi [First Name]—saw you're working on data at [Company]. 
I'm with MotherDuck (serverless warehouse on DuckDB). 
Would love to connect and share ideas.
```

### Template 2: The Content Hook
```
Hi [First Name]—loved your post about [topic]. 
We're thinking about similar problems at MotherDuck. 
Would be great to connect.
```

### Template 3: The Trigger Event
```
Hi [First Name]—congrats on [funding/new role/milestone]! 
I work with data teams at similar-stage companies. 
Happy to connect if useful.
```

### Template 4: The Mutual Connection
```
Hi [First Name]—[Mutual Connection] mentioned you're 
building out the data team at [Company]. Would love to connect.
```

## Post-Connection Message Sequences

### Sequence A: Soft Approach

**Message 1 (After acceptance)**:
```
Thanks for connecting, [First Name]!

Quick question—what's your team using for analytics/data warehouse these days?

(Just curious, not pitching)
```

**Message 2 (If they respond)**:
```
Interesting! We built MotherDuck specifically for teams like yours—
serverless, DuckDB-powered, and way simpler than Snowflake.

Would you be open to a 15-min call to see if it's a fit?
```

**Message 2 (If no response after 5 days)**:
```
No worries if you're busy! If data infrastructure is ever on your radar, 
happy to share how MotherDuck compares to [Snowflake/BigQuery/etc].
```

### Sequence B: Direct Approach

**Message 1 (After acceptance)**:
```
Thanks for connecting!

I noticed [Company] is [growing/hiring data roles/etc]. 

MotherDuck is helping similar teams cut warehouse costs by 70% 
while moving faster—serverless, built on DuckDB.

Worth a quick chat?
```

## InMail Templates

Use InMails for high-priority prospects who haven't accepted connection requests.

### InMail 1: Value-First
**Subject**: Faster analytics for [Company]

**Body**:
```

helping data teams like [Company]'s:

• Cut costs 70% vs. Snowflake
• Eliminate infrastructure management
• Query 4x faster than BigQuery

We're built on DuckDB, so if your team uses Python/SQL, 
you'll feel right at home.

Worth a 15-min call?

[Your Name]
```

**Body**:

## Engagement Strategies

### Before Reaching Out
1. **Like** 2-3 of their posts
2. **Comment** thoughtfully on 1 post
3. **Check** their activity for personalization hooks

### Comment Examples
On a data engineering post:
> "Great point about [topic]. We've seen similar patterns with 
> teams moving to serverless warehouses—the time savings compound fast."

On a hiring announcement:
> "Congrats on the growth! Exciting to see data teams scaling."

### Voice Message
LinkedIn voice messages have higher open rates:
```
"Hey [First Name], [Your Name] here from MotherDuck. 
Saw you're heading up data at [Company]—we help teams 
like yours analyze data faster with zero infrastructure. 
Would love to connect if you have a minute. Talk soon!"
```

## LinkedIn Sales Navigator Tips

### Saved Searches to Create
1. **Data Engineers** at companies 50-500 employees
2. **Analytics Managers** at Series A-C startups
3. **CTOs** at companies 20-100 employees
4. **New hires** in data roles (last 90 days)

### Lead Filters
- Title: Data Engineer, Analytics Engineer, Head of Data
- Company size: 11-500
- Industry: Technology, SaaS, Fintech
- Posted on LinkedIn: Last 30 days (active users)

### Account Filters
- Company headcount growth: 10%+ (growing)
- Technologies used: Snowflake, dbt, Python (if available)
- Funding: Series A-C

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Personalize every message
- Engage with their content first
- Keep messages under 100 words
- Follow up (but not too much)
- Accept that some won't respond

### Don't
- Send generic copy-paste messages
- Pitch immediately after connecting
- Send multiple messages same day
- Use pushy or salesy language
- Give up after one attempt
