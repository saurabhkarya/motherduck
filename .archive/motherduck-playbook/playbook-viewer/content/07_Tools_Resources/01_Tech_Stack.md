## Core Tools

### CRM (Salesforce/HubSpot)

**What to Log (Every Time)**:
- Contact information
- Company details
- Conversation notes
- Next steps
- Lead score/stage

**Best Practices**:
- Update immediately after calls (not end of day)
- Use consistent naming conventions
- Tag leads by source, persona, industry
- Set follow-up tasks with specific dates

**Time-Saving Tips**:
- Use templates for common notes
- Bulk update similar records
- Create saved views for your territory

---

### Sales Engagement (Outreach/Salesloft/Apollo)

**Sequence Best Practices**:
- Keep sequences under 14 days
- Mix channels: email, phone, LinkedIn
- A/B test subject lines
- Pause sequences for hot leads (manual outreach)

**Personalization Variables**:
- {{first_name}}
- {{company}}
- {{industry}}
- {{trigger_event}} (custom)
- {{pain_point}} (custom)

**Performance Metrics to Track**:
| Metric | Target |
|--------|--------|
| Open rate | > 40% |
| Reply rate | > 5% |
| Meeting rate | > 2% |

---

### LinkedIn Sales Navigator

**Must-Have Searches**:
1. Data Engineers at target companies
2. Analytics roles, newly hired
3. Founders at seed-stage startups
4. CTOs at companies 20-200

**Daily LinkedIn Routine**:
- [ ] Check alerts for saved leads
- [ ] Review new connections
- [ ] Engage with 3-5 prospect posts
- [ ] Send 5-10 connection requests
- [ ] Send 3-5 InMails/messages

**Profile Views**:
- Review who viewed your profile
- These are warm leads—reach out

### Data Enrichment (ZoomInfo/Apollo/Clearbit)

**Use For**:
- Finding contact info
- Company firmographics
- Tech stack data
- Intent signals

**Data to Capture**:
- Direct email
- Phone number
- Title and seniority
- Company size
- Funding stage
- Technologies used

---

### Calendar & Scheduling (Calendly/Chili Piper)

**Setup Tips**:
- Block focus time for prospecting
- Offer limited time slots (not wide open)
- Include prep time buffers
- Set up reminders (24h, 1h before)

**Meeting Types**:
- 15 min: Intro/discovery
- 30 min: Deeper discovery
- 45-60 min: Demo (AE-led)

## Daily Workflow

### Morning (30 min)
- [ ] Review calendar and prepare for calls
- [ ] Check CRM for follow-up tasks
- [ ] Review overnight inbound leads
- [ ] Prioritize today's outreach

### Power Hours (2-3 hours)
- [ ] Make calls (aim for 30-50 dials)
- [ ] Send personalized emails (20-30)
- [ ] LinkedIn outreach (10-15)

### Afternoon (1-2 hours)
- [ ] Follow up on responses
- [ ] Research new accounts
- [ ] Update CRM notes
- [ ] Prep for tomorrow

### End of Day (15 min)
- [ ] Log all activities
- [ ] Send scheduled emails
- [ ] Review metrics
- [ ] Plan tomorrow's priorities

## Common Tool Mistakes

| Mistake | Impact | Fix |
|---------|--------|-----|
| Not logging calls | Lost data, bad forecasting | Log immediately |
| Generic sequences | Low response rates | Personalize every message |
| Over-relying on automation | Seems robotic | Add human touches |
| Ignoring data | Missed patterns | Review metrics weekly |
| Tool overload | Distraction | Master core tools first |
