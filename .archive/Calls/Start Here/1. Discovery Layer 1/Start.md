**The Story**
Founder was founding engineer at Big Query for 10+ years and saw what needed to change.
Data warehouses were designed assuming you'd always query massive amounts of data at once
Reality: even if you have terabytes of data, you usually only query a small portion at a time
Modern hardware is powerful enough to handle these real-world queries on a single machine—no need to split across hundreds of servers

Now hardware performance is great.
The reality is even with TBs of data, queries are much smaller than worst-case scenarios.
DuckDB stepped into process locally on performant hardware instead of distbtruting.
Extremely efficient by design, analyses in your machine.
For larger datasets, sharing and collaboration, you need the cloud.

We are faster than Snowflake, ClickHouse .etc according to every benchmark even ClickHouse's own study, and a quarter of the price,

**3 Pillars:**
Severless: We handle the scaling/management 0 you don't architect DuckDB infrastructure yourself.
Single-node: Incredibly fast queries without distributed complexity, no data shuffling between nodes.
Flexible scaling: You can scale nodes up (Standard -> Jumbo -> Mega -> Giga) or scale OUT (pulse instances for many concurrent users)

**Cost**
WE only charge on compute time, not data scanned
Spin up/cool down is fast due to being single node architecutre
