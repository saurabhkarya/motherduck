# Complexity Pain
**When they mention: "It's complex" / "Too many moving parts"**

---

## Acknowledge & Dig Deeper
```
"Who understands your data flow end-to-end?"

If that person was out for a week, what would break?
How long does it take someone new to feel productive?
```

---

## Follow-Up Questions
- "How many tools are in the stack?"
- "Who maintains the infrastructure?"
- "What's the onboarding time for new analysts?"

---

## Impact Framing
The result of complexity:
- Tribal knowledge
- Key person risk
- Onboarding pain
- Fear of breaking things

**Reframe:**
```
"When only a few people understand the system, it becomes a bottleneck, not a tool."
```

---

## What MotherDuck Does
```
"We can provide a simple SQL-first analytics model with fewer moving parts."
```

**What it changes:**
- Lower cognitive load → faster onboarding → less dependency on specialists
