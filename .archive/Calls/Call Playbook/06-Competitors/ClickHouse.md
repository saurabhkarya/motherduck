# Handling ClickHouse
**When they mention ClickHouse as an alternative**

---

## Acknowledge
```
"That's a common comparison."
```

---

## Main Differentiators
The main things we hear from people who left ClickHouse:

1. **Joins** - We handle joins much better (ClickHouse struggles with complex joins)
2. **SQL Syntax** - Our SQL is standard and easier to adopt (ClickHouse has its own dialect)
3. **Cost** - We're more cost-efficient (happy to show benchmarks)

---

## Script
```
"The main things we hear from people who came from ClickHouse:
we handle joins better, standard SQL, and more cost-efficient."

"What's your timeline on making a decision?"
```

---

## Timeline Question
Always ask about timeline to gauge urgency:
```
"What's your timeline on making a decision?"
```

**If soon:** Set up side-by-side comparison
**If longer:** Book intro call to stay top of mind

---

## Benchmark Reference
We're faster than ClickHouse according to every benchmark - even ClickHouse's own study.
