# Handling Snowflake
**When they use or mention Snowflake**

---

## Acknowledge
```
"Snowflake's a solid product - a lot of our customers came from
Snowflake actually."
```

---

## Why Teams Switch
Main reasons teams switch from Snowflake:
1. **Cost** - We're typically 70% cheaper (pay when queries run - no idle costs)
2. **Simplicity** - No warehouse sizing, no cluster tuning

```
"How's Snowflake working for you cost-wise?"
```

---

## Landmine Questions
Plant these to surface pain:
- "How much do you pay when no one's querying?"
- "Who's responsible for warehouse sizing and tuning?"
- "What happens when someone leaves a warehouse running?"

---

## Key Differentiators
| Snowflake | MotherDuck |
|-----------|------------|
| Pay for warehouse uptime | Pay only when queries run |
| Manual warehouse sizing | Serverless - auto scales |
| Distributed architecture | Single-node (faster for most queries) |
| Complex pricing | Simple, predictable |

---

## Not Asking to Replace
```
"I'm not suggesting you rip anything out."

"I'm just saying let's look for workloads that don't belong there.
Are there any queries you avoid running because of cost or speed?"
```
