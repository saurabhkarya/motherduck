# Workloads Limited by Cost
**Discovery question for quantifying cost impact**

---

## When They Confirm They've Limited Something
```
"So cost is preventing growth, not just annoying you."

What use case are you not pursuing because of cost concerns?
I want to understand what you would do if cost wasn't a constraint.
```

---

## If They've Removed Historical Data
```
"That's a serious trade-off. So you're making product decisions
based on warehouse cost."

"If you could bring the historical data back without blowing the budget - would you?"
```

---

## The Reframe
```
"We don't want you making business decisions based on warehouse cost
instead of what's best for the business."
```
