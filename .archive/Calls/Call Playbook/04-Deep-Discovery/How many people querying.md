# How Many People Are Querying
**Discovery question for understanding scale**

---

## The Question
```
"How many people are actively querying the data?"
```

---

## When They Give a Number
```
"Got it - so X people are power users actively querying."

"In our model, each user would get their dedicated compute instance.
That eliminates the resource contention where queries queue up,
and gives you more control over cost since each user/team has their own sizing."
```

---

## Why This Matters
- Few users (1-10): Perfect fit, simple setup
- Medium (10-50): Still great fit, can show cost efficiency
- Large (50+): Highlight read scaling features on Business Plan
