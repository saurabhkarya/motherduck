# Competitor Close
**Use when they're evaluating alternatives**

---

## The Offer
```
"Typically what I'd recommend is running a free 3-week trial where you
can compare us side-by-side with [competitor] or any other options you're considering."
```

---

## The Support
```
"I'll put you in a shared Slack channel with our engineering team."

"They'll help you load the data, optimize the queries, and run benchmarks."
```

---

## The Ask
```
"Does that sound like something worth doing?"
```

---

## Lock the Calendar
```
"Let me get you on the calendar with our team to kick off the trial."

"Are you in front of your laptop? I'll send you the invite now."
```

---

## What They Get
- Free 3-week trial
- Side-by-side comparison
- Shared Slack channel with engineering
- Help with:
  - Loading data
  - Optimizing queries
  - Running benchmarks
- Real cost data to compare

---

## After 3 Weeks
```
"After 3 weeks you'll have real cost data to compare."

"Does that sound worthwhile?"
```
