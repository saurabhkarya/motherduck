# Speed Pain
**When they mention: "It's slow" / "Queries take forever"**

---

## Acknowledge & Dig Deeper
```
"What part usually takes the longest?"

Are we talking seconds, minutes, or longer?
Is it all queries or specific types - joins, aggregations, large tables?
```

---

## Follow-Up Questions
- "What happens when people are waiting?"
- "Do people abandon questions or skip follow-up analysis?"
- "How does that affect decision-making speed?"

---

## Impact Framing
The result of slow queries:
- Context switching
- Abandoned questions
- Fewer follow-ups
- Worse decisions

**Reframe:**
```
"Most teams don't realize how much speed they've given up until they get it back."
```
