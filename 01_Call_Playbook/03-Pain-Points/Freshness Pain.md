# Freshness Pain
**When they mention: "Data is stale" / "Nightly refreshes"**

---

## Dig Into the Impact
```
"What decisions are being made on yesterday's data?"

What would change if the data was available sooner?
Is this blocking AI/ML initiatives or experimentation?
```

---

## Impact Framing
The result of stale data:
- Delayed decisions
- Missed opportunities
- AI/Experimentation blockers

**Reframe:**
```
"It's not about real-time - it's about relevance."
```
