# Cost Pre-Close
**Use after discovering cost pain**

---

## The Reframe
```
"We don't want you making business decisions based on warehouse cost
instead of what's best for the business."

"Our pricing model is fundamentally different - we don't charge per query,
we charge by second of compute uptime."

"Analysts can run as many queries as they need without racking up cost."
```

---

## Concrete Example
```
"Let me give you a more concrete example:"

"Let's say you're under 2TB - you'll end up spending $200-800 for full
analytics workloads. That includes platform fee, compute, and storage."

"With [competitor], you're likely spending in the thousands or tens of thousands."
```
