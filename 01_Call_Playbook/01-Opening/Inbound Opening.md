
[[Voicemail]]
# Inbound Opening
**For leads who signed up or downloaded content**

---

## Script
```
Hey [Name], this is Arya from MotherDuck. How have you been?

I saw you [signed up for an account / downloaded our guide] - what are you working on at the moment?
```

---

## Permission Bridge (if needed)
- "Do you have 30 seconds?"
- "Is now a terrible time?"
- "Can I steal a quick minute?"
