# Slow AND Expensive
**Response: "It's both slow and expensive"**

---

## Acknowledge the Pain
```
"That's the worst combo - expensive AND slow."
```

---

## Quantify Both
```
"How long are we talking - seconds, minutes, or longer?"

"Is it all queries or specific types - joins, aggregations, large tables?"
```

---

## Get the Numbers
```
"How much data are we talking about here - ballpark terabytes?"

"How many people are actively querying this data?"
```

---

## This is a Strong Signal
When you hear both cost AND speed issues, this is a high-quality opportunity.

**Reframe:**
```
"Most teams don't realize how much speed they've given up until they get it back."

"And cost only becomes a problem when it changes how people behave."
```
