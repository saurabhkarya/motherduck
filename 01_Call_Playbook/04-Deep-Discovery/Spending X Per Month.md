# When They Know Their Spend
**Response: "We're spending $X per month and it keeps growing"**

---

## Acknowledge & Dig Deeper
```
"Okay, so you're spending $X and it's trending up - that's helpful context."

Help me understand what's driving growth - is it more users querying,
more data volume, or specific workloads that are expensive to run?

What would acceptable cost look like for you? Cut in half or just stop growth?
```

---

## The Sweet Spot Reframe
If their data is under 10TB:
```
"That's interesting - if you're under [data size] and spending [$X],
that doesn't quite add up - and that's actually a sweet spot for us."

"We see this a lot where the per-query model gets expensive with
moderate data volumes."

"We charge by second of compute uptime, not per query. So exploratory
analysis doesn't cost you extra."
```

---

## Show the Value
```
"I'd love to show you what pricing would look like for your use case."

"How are queries running speed-wise?"
```
