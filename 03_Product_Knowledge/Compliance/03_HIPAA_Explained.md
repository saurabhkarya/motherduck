# HIPAA Compliance: What It Means for Sales

This document explains HIPAA compliance in the context of selling MotherDuck to healthcare and health-tech customers.

---

## What is HIPAA?

**HIPAA** (Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act) is a US federal law that protects sensitive patient health information. It sets standards for how healthcare organizations and their vendors handle Protected Health Information (PHI).

### Key HIPAA Components

| Component | What It Covers |
|-----------|----------------|
| **Privacy Rule** | How PHI can be used and disclosed |
| **Security Rule** | Technical safeguards for electronic PHI |
| **Breach Notification** | Requirements if PHI is compromised |
| **Enforcement Rule** | Penalties for violations |

---

## What is PHI?

**Protected Health Information (PHI)** includes any health data that can identify an individual:

### Examples of PHI
- Patient names, addresses, birth dates
- Medical record numbers
- Health conditions and diagnoses
- Treatment information
- Insurance information
- Any combination that could identify a patient

### What's NOT PHI
- De-identified/anonymized health data
- Aggregate statistics (no individual identification)
- Employee health plan administration data (different rules)

---

## Who Needs HIPAA Compliance?

### Covered Entities
- Healthcare providers (hospitals, clinics, doctors)
- Health insurance plans
- Healthcare clearinghouses

### Business Associates
Any vendor that handles PHI on behalf of a covered entity:
- Cloud service providers (like MotherDuck)
- Analytics platforms
- Data warehouses
- IT service providers

**If your prospect stores or processes patient data, they likely need HIPAA-compliant vendors.**

## MotherDuck's HIPAA Status

### What MotherDuck Offers

- ✅ **HIPAA BAA Available** - Business Associate Agreement on request
- ✅ **Business Plan Required** - BAA only for Business Plan customers
- ✅ **SOC 2 Type II** - Security controls validated
- ✅ **Encryption** - Data encrypted at rest and in transit

### The Business Associate Agreement (BAA)

A BAA is a legal contract required by HIPAA when a vendor handles PHI. It:
- Defines how PHI will be protected
- Establishes vendor responsibilities
- Limits how PHI can be used
- Requires breach notification

**Without a signed BAA, a healthcare company cannot legally store PHI with a vendor.**
