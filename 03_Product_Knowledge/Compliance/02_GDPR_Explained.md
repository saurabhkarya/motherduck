# GDPR Compliance: What It Means for Sales

This document explains GDPR compliance in the context of selling MotherDuck to European customers and companies handling EU resident data.

---

## What is GDPR?

**GDPR** (General Data Protection Regulation) is a comprehensive data privacy law enacted by the European Union in 2018. It governs how organizations collect, store, process, and protect personal data of EU residents.

### Key GDPR Principles

| Principle | What It Means |
|-----------|---------------|
| **Lawfulness** | Data must be processed legally and transparently |
| **Purpose Limitation** | Collect data only for specified, legitimate purposes |
| **Data Minimization** | Collect only data that's necessary |
| **Accuracy** | Keep personal data accurate and up to date |
| **Storage Limitation** | Don't keep data longer than needed |
| **Security** | Protect data against unauthorized processing |
| **Accountability** | Organizations must demonstrate compliance |

---

## Who Does GDPR Apply To?

### Companies That Must Comply

GDPR applies if your prospect:

1. **Is located in the EU/EEA** - Any company in Europe
2. **Processes EU resident data** - Even if based elsewhere
3. **Offers goods/services to EU** - E-commerce, SaaS, etc.
4. **Monitors EU resident behavior** - Analytics, tracking

### Practical Examples

| Scenario | GDPR Applies? |
|----------|---------------|
| UK startup with EU customers | ✅ Yes |
| US company with EU users | ✅ Yes |
| German company, German customers | ✅ Yes |
| US company, US customers only | ❌ No |

## The Data Processing Agreement (DPA)

### What is a DPA?

A DPA is a legally binding contract between:
- **Data Controller** (your customer) - Determines how data is used
- **Data Processor** (MotherDuck) - Processes data on their behalf

### Why Customers Need It

Under GDPR, companies must have a DPA with any vendor that processes personal data on their behalf. It's not optional.

### How to Handle DPA Requests

**Prospect**: "We need a DPA signed."

**Response**:
```
"Absolutely—we have a standard Data Processing Agreement ready to go. 
I can have our team send it over for your review. Most customers 
sign the standard DPA, but we can discuss any specific requirements.

Want me to initiate that process?"
```

**Contact**: security@motherduck.com for DPA requests

## How to Use This in Sales Conversations

### When They Mention EU/Europe

**Prospect**: "We have customers in Europe."

**Response**:
```
"Then GDPR compliance is important for you. Good news—MotherDuck 
is GDPR verified, and we have an EU data region in Frankfurt. 
Your EU data can stay in Europe.

We also provide a Data Processing Agreement that covers our

```

### When They Ask About Data Residency

**Prospect**: "Where is our data stored?"

**Response**:
```

For EU compliance, we have a Frankfurt (eu-central-1) region. 
Data stored there stays in Europe.

Currently, organizations are single-region, so all your data 
stays in your chosen location."
```

**Prospect**: "We have specific DPA requirements from our legal team."

**Response**:
```
"We have a standard DPA that covers most requirements. I'd suggest 
we send it over for your legal team to review. If there are specific 
provisions you need, we can discuss those.

What's typically the turnaround on your legal review?"
```

## GDPR Qualification Questions

Ask these to gauge GDPR relevance:

1. "Do you have customers or users in the EU?"
2. "Where is your company headquartered?"
3. "Do you need to keep EU data within Europe?"
4. "Will you need a Data Processing Agreement?"
5. "Is your legal/compliance team involved in vendor decisions?"

## Common Questions & Answers

### "Is MotherDuck GDPR compliant?"

> "Yes—we're GDPR verified by GDPR Local. We can provide a Data Processing Agreement, and we have an EU data region so your data can stay in Europe."

### "Can we keep data in the EU?"

> "Absolutely. Our EU region is in Frankfurt. When you create your organization, you choose the region, and all your data stays there."

### "What's the process for data subject requests?"

> "If someone exercises their GDPR rights (like requesting deletion), you control your data and can fulfill those requests directly. We process data on your behalf per the DPA."

### "Do you transfer data outside the EU?"

> "For EU region customers, data stays in Frankfurt. No transatlantic transfers required. This simplifies your compliance significantly."

### "How do we get the DPA?"

> "Just email security@motherduck.com or let me know, and I'll have our team send it over. It's a straightforward process."

## GDPR Red Flags

Watch for these—may require extra conversation:

| Signal | What It Means |
|--------|---------------|
| "We're in healthcare with EU patients" | GDPR + potentially more |
| "Our legal team is strict" | Longer sales cycle |
| "We've been fined before" | Extra cautious, high scrutiny |
| "We need custom DPA terms" | Legal review, potential delays |
